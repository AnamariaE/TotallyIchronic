Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Totally iChronic Publisher is a personal tool used by a single creator (Anamaría Espinoza) to publish her own videos to her own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data is accessed

When the creator authorizes the app, it receives basic profile information (display name and avatar) and permission to upload and publish videos to that same account.

How data is used

That access is used only to upload and publish the creator's own videos. Nothing else is done with it.

Sharing and storage

We do not sell, share or disclose data to third parties, and we do not collect data from any other person. Authorization tokens are kept by the connection provider (Composio) only to keep the connection working.

Revoking access

The creator can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services).
